# My Role in the Grand Scheme

Welcome to New Caelum.

You're a newly licensed private investigator. Gather clues, interrogate
suspects, search crime scenes and connect the pieces (and perhaps defend
yourself). Every case is a puzzle, and every conclusion is yours to make.

Take cases. Get paid. Stay alive. Repeat.

Build your investigation board, establish relationships between people, places
and evidence, and uncover the madness and the corruption hiding beneath the
city's surface.

There won’t be any easy answers.

Figure it out yourself.

P.S. Keep in mind that the game is VERY difficult, the tutorial is minimal, and
you'll have to learn as you go (everything is intentional).
