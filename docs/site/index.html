Open-source desktop karaoke, without the cloud lock-in.

Turn your own music library into a cleaner, calmer karaoke setup.

OpenKara keeps the heavy work on your machine: stem separation, synced lyrics, portable libraries, and playback tuned for real songs you already own.

Download releases Install guide

Built for local-first karaoke

  • On-device AI Separate vocals and accompaniment without uploading tracks.
  • Synced lyrics Use LRCLIB, embedded tags, or sidecar `.lrc` files.
  • Portable library Keep songs, cache, and metadata in one self-contained folder.

What makes it useful

A small stack for people who want control, not a subscription.

Local audio import

Bring your existing library, not a walled garden.

Import the tracks you already own, browse them quickly, and keep metadata and cached stems close to the source files.

2-stem and 4-stem modes

Choose the right level of separation for the song.

Start with vocals plus accompaniment, or upgrade to separate drums, bass, and other instruments when you want more control.

Cross-platform desktop

macOS, Windows, and Linux.

OpenKara ships as a Tauri desktop app and keeps the interface fast, compact, and practical for everyday playback.

Details

Designed to stay lightweight.

Lyrics that adapt to your files

OpenKara can use online synced lyrics, embedded tags, or nearby `.lrc` files.

Resumable separation

Chunk-level checkpoints let the app continue where it left off after interruption.

Model download on first launch

The Demucs ONNX model is downloaded automatically when the app needs it.
