Open-source desktop karaoke, built for your own music.

Turn your music library into a karaoke stage.

OpenKara removes vocals from songs you already own, adds synced lyrics, and lets you sing along — all running on your computer, no subscription required.

Desktop karaoke from your own music

Your own music

Bring your own files.

Use songs you already own — MP3, FLAC, WAV, or AAC. No subscription, no catalog limits.

Portable library

Take it anywhere.

Your entire karaoke library lives in one folder you can copy to a USB drive, NAS, or another computer.

Simple playback

Just sing.

Lyrics scroll in sync, volume controls stay out of the way, and everything works without an internet connection.

What it does

Everything you need to turn any song into karaoke.

Simple enough to get singing in minutes, with deeper controls available when you want them.

AI vocal removal

Remove vocals right on your computer.

Start with a simple vocals-off mode, or go further with individual control over drums, bass, and other instruments.

Synced lyrics

Lyrics appear and scroll in time with the music.

OpenKara finds lyrics online automatically. It also picks up lyrics already embedded in your files or saved alongside them.

4-stem mixer

Adjust each instrument independently.

Separate volume controls for vocals, drums, bass, and other instruments — turn down what you don't want, keep what you do.

Resumable processing

Pick up where you left off.

Close the app mid-separation and it continues from where it stopped next time — no need to start over.

Cross-platform

Works on macOS, Windows, and Linux.

Same app, same library format. Move between computers without converting or re-processing anything.

Local setup for contributors

  • Node.js 20+
  • pnpm 10+
  • Rust stable toolchain
  • Tauri 2 platform prerequisites
git clone https://github.com/thedavidweng/OpenKara.git
cd OpenKara
pnpm install
./scripts/setup.sh
pnpm tauri dev
