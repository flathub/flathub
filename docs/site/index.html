Local-first desktop karaoke for the music you already own.

Turn your music library into a calmer karaoke setup.

OpenKara removes vocals from songs you already own, adds synced lyrics, and keeps the whole workflow on your computer. No subscription, no cloud-first catalog, no need to repurchase music.

What it does

Built to feel straightforward, even when the audio work is not.

The goal is not to look like a DJ console. It is to help you get from a normal music file to a singable track with as little friction as possible.

AI vocal removal

Remove vocals locally, then go deeper only if you want to.

Start with a simple vocals-off mode, or move into 4-stem control for drums, bass, and other instruments when a song needs more shaping.

Synced lyrics

Use online lyrics, embedded tags, or sidecar `.lrc` files.

OpenKara looks up timed lyrics automatically, but it also respects the lyric data that already lives with your files.

Resumable processing

Stop halfway through and continue later without starting over.

Longer processing jobs resume from checkpoints, so closing the app does not mean throwing away the work already done.

Cross-platform

Keep the same library across macOS, Windows, and Linux.

The library stays self-contained, so you can move between computers without rebuilding everything from scratch.
